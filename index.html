About Skiptracing

What Is A Skiptrace

Skiptracing is the process of locating individuals whose contact information is unknown or out of date. Used across debt collection, real estate, legal matters and law enforcement, it draws on a wide range of tools and databases to establish a person's whereabouts.

That can mean a current residential address, a working phone number, an email, an employer or other public record information that lets you make contact and move your matter forward.

  • Address verification and reverse locates
  • Full locates on hard to find subjects
  • Bulk ledger tracing for lenders and agencies
